How to Export a WhatsApp Chat

WhatsApp can export any conversation — individual or group — as a plain text file. The export takes under a minute and is all ChatWrapped needs to build your recap. Follow the steps for your platform.

Android

  1. Open WhatsApp and open the chat you want to export.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right corner).
  3. Choose More → Export chat.
  4. Choose Without media — this produces the plain .txt file ChatWrapped parses fastest. “Include media” produces a ZIP of images plus the same TXT.
  5. Save or share the file to your Downloads, Google Drive, or directly to your computer.

iPhone

  1. Open WhatsApp and open the chat.
  2. Tap the contact or group name at the top of the conversation.
  3. Scroll down and tap Export Chat.
  4. Choose Without Media.
  5. Save the file with the iOS share sheet — “Save to Files” is the easiest way to find it later.

Tips & answers to common questions

Which option should I pick — with or without media?

For ChatWrapped, pick Without Media. Media ZIPs can be hundreds of megabytes and the images don’t participate in text analytics anyway (attachment placeholders are counted as media events either way).

Does the export include deleted messages?

Deleted messages appear only as markers like “This message was deleted”. ChatWrapped classifies them as system events and excludes them from text statistics — the content is gone from WhatsApp itself.

Where is my export on the phone?

Android usually saves to Download/WhatsApp or opens a share dialog; iPhone shows the share sheet immediately. You can also email the file to yourself and continue on a computer.

Can I export several chats?

Yes — repeat the steps per chat. ChatWrapped can hold multiple conversations in one session and compare them side by side.

The export looks garbled in a text editor

WhatsApp TXT exports contain Unicode directionality marks and emoji — that’s normal. Don’t edit the file; drop it into the analyzer as-is. If dates look wrong after import, use the DD/MM ↔ MM/DD toggle on the import result screen.

Is exporting allowed? Is it private?

Exporting your own chats is a standard WhatsApp feature. The file stays on your device unless you choose to share it — and if you bring it to ChatWrapped, it is parsed locally in your browser and never uploaded.
