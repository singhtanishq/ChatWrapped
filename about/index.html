About ChatWrapped

ChatWrapped started with a simple observation: our most meaningful conversations happen in chat apps, and then they scroll away. “Wrapped”-style recaps proved that people love seeing their year in data — but your chats deserve the same treatment without handing a stranger’s server your most personal files.

What it is

A free, open-source, browser-based tool that turns WhatsApp and Telegram exports into accurate statistics, personalized story recaps and social-ready share cards. It runs entirely as a static site — there is no backend, no database and no account system, which is also why it can never lose your data: it never has it.

Principles

  • Local by architecture, not by promise. Nothing to upload to, by design — verifiable in the network tab and by automated tests.
  • Honest numbers. Every metric is documented with definitions, exclusions and edge cases. Ties are labeled; missing data is admitted.
  • Fun without manipulation. Stories and awards are playful activity facts — never fake streak-pressure or psychological claims.
  • Free forever for the core. Static hosting on GitHub Pages costs nothing; there are no paid tiers to gate.

Technology

Astro + React with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, hand-rolled SVG charts, a Canvas card renderer, Web Workers for parsing and analytics, JSZip for recap exports, Vitest and Playwright for the test suite — deployed as static files. The full architecture is documented in the repository.

Known limitations

  • Telegram HTML exports and WhatsApp media ZIPs are not parsed yet (JSON and TXT are).
  • Timezone information is absent from exports, so day/hour buckets reflect the exporter’s clock.
  • Story slides adapt to your data — small chats simply produce fewer slides.
  • Very large exports (hundreds of MB) are beyond the tested limits.

Roadmap ideas

  • More platforms (Instagram, Signal, iMessage via exports) as format testing allows.
  • Telegram HTML export support.
  • Additional card layouts and theme refinements.
  • More languages for stopwords and UI.

Contributing

Bug reports, parser fixtures and theme ideas are welcome at https://github.com/singhtanishq/ChatWrapped — see CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository. Please never attach real chat exports to issues; fixtures are synthetic.
