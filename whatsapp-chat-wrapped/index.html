WhatsApp Chat Wrapped

Your WhatsApp conversations already hold a story — message streaks, late-night talks, favorite emoji, who starts every conversation. ChatWrapped turns the official WhatsApp TXT export into that story: accurate statistics, a personalized recap sequence and downloadable share cards. Free, without an account, and processed entirely in your browser.

What you get from a WhatsApp export

  • Message analytics — totals, words, characters, average lengths and per-participant shares.
  • Time insights — monthly trends, hour × weekday heatmap, weekday rhythm, longest active-day streak.
  • Response rhythm — median reply times per participant, measured with a documented burst rule.
  • Emoji & word profiles — grapheme-safe emoji counting and stopword-filtered signature words.
  • A personalized story — 8–15 slides built from your real numbers, with nine visual themes.
  • Share cards — 1080×1920, 1080×1350, 1080×1080 and 1200×630 PNGs, individually or as a ZIP.

Which WhatsApp exports are supported?

ChatWrapped reads the plain-text chat export that WhatsApp itself generates — on Android (“Export chat” in the chat menu, choose without media) and on iPhone (the same option from the contact or group page). Both Android and iOS timestamp styles are parsed automatically, including 12-hour and 24-hour clocks, multiline messages, attachment placeholders and system events like group renames and missed calls.

Not sure how to get the file? Follow the step-by-step WhatsApp export guide — it takes about a minute.

Privacy: your export never leaves your device

Your uploaded conversations are processed entirely in your browser. ChatWrapped does not upload your chat files to its servers — there is no ChatWrapped server that could receive them.

Parsing and analysis run in Web Workers on your device. You can watch the network tab during the whole workflow: the only requests are the static files of the site itself. After you close the tab, nothing remains unless you explicitly saved a recap summary. Read the full privacy policy.

Built for group chats too

Family groups, class groups, friend groups — all supported. Before analyzing you can include or exclude any participant, anonymize names for screenshots, rename people for presentation and restrict the analysis to a date range. See group chat wrapped for the group-specific workflow.

How the numbers stay honest

Every metric has a written definition with its exclusions and edge cases — message share, burst rules for response times, conversation starts, emoji counting and more are documented in how chat analysis works. ChatWrapped reports counting statistics; it never claims they measure affection, loyalty or friendship quality.
