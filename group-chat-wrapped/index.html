Group Chat Wrapped

Group chats are where the chaos lives — twenty people, five time zones, one emoji war. ChatWrapped analyzes WhatsApp and Telegram group exports with per-participant statistics, privacy controls for every member and share cards the whole group can enjoy. Free and processed entirely in your browser.

Analyze a group chat →

Built for many participants

  • Per-participant dashboard — messages, share, words, emoji, media, conversation starts and median reply time in one sortable table.
  • Include / exclude anyone — analyzing “just the three of us from the group” takes one click per participant.
  • Anonymization — replace real names with “Participant A/B/…” before screenshotting; renames are presentation-only.
  • Awards — Night Owl, Conversation Starter, Emoji Enthusiast and five more, each with explicit thresholds and honest ties.
  • Transparent samples — small groups get a “small sample” note instead of overconfident numbers.

The group-specific workflow

  1. Export the group (WhatsApp: “Export chat → without media”; Telegram: JSON export).
  2. Drop the file into the analyzer — every participant is detected automatically.
  3. On the setup screen, decide who to include, who is “you”, and whether to anonymize.
  4. Optionally restrict the analysis to a date range (great for “this semester” or “the trip week”).
  5. Explore the dashboard, play the story, and export cards for the group wall.

System events don’t pollute the numbers

Group exports are full of noise: “X created the group”, “X added Y”, encryption notices, missed calls, deleted message markers. ChatWrapped classifies all of these as system events and keeps them out of ordinary message totals — with a transparent count so you always know what was excluded. Attachment placeholders (“<Media omitted>”) are counted as media events, not as text.

Reading group statistics responsibly

Message frequency is activity, not affection. A quiet member can be the most loyal one — ChatWrapped shows counting statistics and never claims to measure relationships.

That’s why comparisons show sample sizes, awards list explicit thresholds, and ties are labeled as ties. See how chat analysis works for every definition.
