# ChatWrapped

> **Your conversations. Your memories. Your story.**
> Turn your WhatsApp and Telegram conversations into beautiful, shareable recaps. Free, instant and private — everything is processed in your browser, never uploaded.

**Status:** 🚧 under active development — see [docs/](docs/) for architecture and metric definitions. This README will document the full feature set, screenshots and privacy architecture for v1.0.

ChatWrapped is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp, Meta, Telegram, Spotify or any other messaging platform.
