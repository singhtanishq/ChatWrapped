WhatsApp Chat Analyzer

A WhatsApp chat analyzer answers simple questions with real numbers: how many messages, who sends more, when the chat is most alive, which emoji carry the conversation. ChatWrapped is a free analyzer that runs the entire computation locally in your browser — the export file is never uploaded anywhere.

Analyzer features designed for privacy

  • No upload: the parser runs in a Web Worker on your device; no message text is ever transmitted.
  • No account: no sign-up, no email, no cookies for tracking.
  • Anonymization: replace participant names with “Participant A/B/…” before sharing screenshots.
  • Memory-only processing: parsed data lives in the tab; closing it clears everything.
  • Offline capable: after the first visit, the analyzer works without an internet connection.

From analysis to shareable results

Numbers alone are dry, so ChatWrapped turns your analysis into a personalized story sequence and into high-resolution share cards in nine visual themes. Cards export as exact-size PNGs (story 1080×1920, portrait 1080×1350, square 1080×1080, landscape 1200×630) — individually or as a complete ZIP recap.

Why another WhatsApp analyzer?

Existing tools either upload your chat to a server, bury the privacy story, or stop at raw message counts. ChatWrapped is built around three principles: local processing (verifiable in your network tab), documented, reproducible metrics (every number has a written definition), and storytelling quality (themes, animation and card design you actually want to share).

Getting started

  1. Export your WhatsApp chat as TXT (without media).
  2. Open the analyzer and drop the file in.
  3. Pick participants and a date range, then explore the dashboard, story and cards.

Prefer to look first? The analyzer has a clearly-labeled synthetic sample — no export required. Just press “Try a friends chat”.
